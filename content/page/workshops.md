## Featured Recent Workshops

{{< feature-card >}}
### {{< awesome fas fa-university >}} ICPSR Summer Program in Quantitative Methods
**Dates:** July 13–17, 2026 (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM ET) | **Format:** Virtual

* **Course Title:** *Sensitivity Analysis: Quantifying the Robustness of Inferences to Alternative Factors or Data*
* **Overview:** A 20-instruction-hour workshop covering statistical tools (R, Stata, KonFound-It! Shiny App) to quantify the strength of evidence necessary to inform policy, practice, or social science.

#### Key Course Goals
* Understand basic sensitivity analysis frameworks based on omitted variables and potential outcomes.
* Conduct and interpret sensitivity analyses from published outputs.
* Apply sensitivity analysis directly to your own studies.

### {{< awesome fas fa-landmark >}} Association for Public Policy Analysis & Management (APPAM)
**Date:** November 14, 2025 (1:45–3:15 PM PT) | **Location:** Seattle, WA (In Person)

* **Title:** *What Would it Take to Change Your Inference? Informing Collaborative Evidence Generation and Evaluation*
* **Part I:** Using potential outcomes to evaluate how much bias (case replacement) is needed to nullify an effect.
* **Part II:** Quantifying robustness in terms of correlations with unobserved confounding variables.
* **Hands-on Practice:** Module demonstrations using R, Stata, and the KonFound-It! web app.
